(cricket legends)

Virat Kohli was born on 5 November 1988.

He is an Indian international cricketer and former all-format captain of the Indian national cricket team.

He is a right-handed batsman and an occasional right-arm medium pace bowler.

Virat Kohli is considered one of the greatest all-format batsmen in the history of cricket.

He is popularly known as "King", "Chase Master", and "Run Machine".

He has the most centuries in One Day Internationals (ODIs).

He has the second-most centuries in international cricket with 85 centuries across all formats.

He is also the leading run-scorer in the Indian Premier League (IPL).

Kohli is the most successful Test captain of India with the most wins.

He led India to retain the Test mace three consecutive times.

He is the only batsman to achieve 900+ rating points in all three formats of cricket.



Mahendra Singh Dhoni was born on 7 July 1981.

He is a former Indian international cricketer and one of the greatest captains in the history of cricket.

He was the captain of the Indian national team in all three formats of the game.

Dhoni is a right-handed batsman and a wicket-keeper.

He is popularly known as “Captain Cool” for his calm and composed leadership on the field.

He led India to win the ICC 20 World Cup in 2007.

He also captained India to victory in the ICC Cricket World Cup in 2011.

Under his captaincy, India won the ICC Champions Trophy in 2013.

He invented the helicopter shoot



AB de Villiers is a former South African international cricketer.

His full name is Abraham Benjamin de Villiers.

He was born on 17 February 1984 in Pretoria, South Africa.

He is known as “Mr. 360” because he can play shots in all directions.

He played for the South Africa national cricket team from 2004 to 2018.

In 2015 he scored the fastest ODI century in just 31 balls against West Indies.

He also played in the Indian Premier League for Royal Challengers Bangalore.

AB de Villiers is famous for his powerful batting and innovative shots.

He is considered one of the greatest and most entertaining batsmen in modern cricket.



Chris Gayle is a famous Jamaican cricketer and one of the most powerful batsmen in the history of cricket.

His full name is Christopher Henry Gayle.

He was born on 21 September 1979 in Kingston, Jamaica.

He played international cricket for the West Indies cricket team.

Chris Gayle is known for his aggressive batting style and big sixes.

He is often called the “Universe Boss” by his fans.

He is the first batsman to score a double century in the ICC Cricket World Cup.

Gayle has played in many T20 leagues including the Indian Premier League.

In IPL he played for teams like Royal Challengers Bangalore and Kings XI Punjab.

He is considered one of the greatest T20 batsmen in cricket history.



Sachin Tendulkar is a legendary Indian cricketer and one of the greatest batsmen in the history of cricket.

His full name is Sachin Ramesh Tendulkar.

He was born on 24 April 1973 in Mumbai, India.

He played international cricket for the India national cricket team for more than 24 years.

Sachin Tendulkar is popularly known as the “God of Cricket”.

He is the first player to score 100 international centuries.

He scored more than 34,000 runs in international cricket.

He was also a part of the Indian team that won the ICC Cricket World Cup in 2011.

In 2014, he received the Bharat Ratna, the highest civilian award in India.

Sachin Tendulkar is respected all over the world for his talent, dedication, and sportsmanship
